About

AI that fits the way your business works.

Carlton Davis Consulting helps businesses put AI to work in ways that are clear, useful, and tied to real business needs. The company provides workshops, training, speaking, and advisory support for leaders and teams who want to use AI with more confidence in daily operations.

The focus is not on hype or technical complexity. It is on helping organizations identify where AI fits, improve how work gets done, build responsible habits, and understand how new tools can support better decisions and execution.

Every engagement is designed to help people move from interest or uncertainty to clearer, more capable AI use.

Meet Carlton

Experience that keeps AI practical.

Carlton Davis is a business founder, four-time COO, Integrator, and AI consultant who has helped organizations grow from startup stage to $87 million in annual revenue. His background spans marketing, software, engineering, logistics, and retail, with leadership experience across teams ranging from 10 to 600 people.

Before focusing on AI, Carlton spent decades solving operational challenges, scaling teams, improving processes, and helping organizations execute through change. That experience shapes his approach to AI: practical, plain-English, and grounded in how businesses actually work.

Based in Nashville, Carlton is active in the local AI and entrepreneurial community. He advises founders through the Nashville Entrepreneur Center, serves on the Nashville Chamber of Commerce AI Innovation Committee, and has spoken for conferences, associations, leadership groups, boards of directors, and business events on practical AI adoption and the future of AI in business.

Carlton has completed generative AI training and hands-on coursework across leading platforms including ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, and other AI tools.
